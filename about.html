We at Osta Trading pride ourselves on offering support to our clients throughout the entire supply process. From manufacturer and product sourcing, to providing logistics solution, warehousing and regulatory support. Thanks to our immense experience inside the pharmaceutical market, we were able to establish a network of not only European, but also global manufacturers. This results in our ability to offer our clients flexible solutions that are tailored to each client's individual technical, regulatory or/and commercial requirements. We don't just supply, we deliver our best.

Our business aims to provide every one of our clients with professional pharmaceutical distribution, international sourcing and reliable logistics. Whether our clients require a product for an established commercial project or are searching for a new product for a new developing project, our highly experienced team will assist you with any request, guaranteeing to offer high-quality products at competitive prices.
