Supply & Logistics

We organise supply
from the manufacturer
to your door

You get deliveries organised to your requirements, from the manufacturer to your destination. We take into account the handling, documentation and transport requirements of pharmaceutical ingredients. Our warehouse in Reinbek lets us consolidate supplies and keep stock for selected products; where appropriate, we set up safety stocks and supply arrangements based on agreed forecasts and expected consumption.

Together with logistics partners experienced in pharmaceutical and other chemical products, we coordinate international transport, import and export procedures and the shipping documents.
